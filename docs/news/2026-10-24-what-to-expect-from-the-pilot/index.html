What to expect from the early-access pilot

What the pilot is planned to cover, what is still undecided, and where each question is answered.

24 October 2026

The early-access pilot is not open. When it opens, sign-up opens with it, and new accounts are planned to get $10 of promotional credit with no card needed to start. Nothing here is a promise of a date.

What is planned

  • One adventure, or one bounded free-roam experience, for two to six players depending on what testing supports. We will state the largest party we have actually tested.
  • A host who sets up the table, friends who join from their phones, and a shared budget the table controls. How it works walks through the four steps.
  • Saving and resuming a campaign, so the same heroes and story pick up next time.

What is still open

  • Prices are proposed and have not been approved. They are on the pricing page and may change.
  • The rules are a subset of the SRD, starting with a level 1 Fighter. See the rules page.
  • The terms, privacy, refund, credit and content policies are drafts that have not been reviewed by counsel. They are linked from the footer of every page and are not in effect.

When something on this list changes, we will post it here and add it to the changelog.
