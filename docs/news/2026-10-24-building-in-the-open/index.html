Building DungeonFlux in the open

What DungeonFlux is, where it stands today, and where to follow along while the early-access pilot is prepared.

24 October 2026

DungeonFlux is an AI Dungeon Master for a living-room table. The adventure plays on a shared TV or laptop, and each player joins from their own phone, tablet or laptop. A rules engine on the server rolls the dice and applies the rules, which follow the System Reference Document 5.2.1. An AI storyteller voices the people you meet and describes what the dice decided.

Where it stands

A local game is playable from start to finish on a development machine. There is no packaged release, no hosted service and no accounts yet. We are preparing an early-access pilot that people can join from a browser. It is not open yet, and we have not set a date.

The rules the game runs are a growing subset of the SRD. The rules page lists what is built so far, what is partly built and what is not built yet.

Follow along

  • The source is on GitHub, including the project history.
  • The changelog lists changes a player would notice, grouped by date.
  • News posts appear on this page when there is something true to say.
