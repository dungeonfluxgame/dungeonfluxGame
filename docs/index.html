What your Dungeon Master does all night

The story

Characters with voices and secrets

Every innkeeper, merchant and villain speaks in a voice of their own, with a personality, a past and things they would rather not say. Charm them, haggle with them, threaten them. They remember how you treated them.

The rules

Real rules. Fair dice. Nobody fudges.

Fifth-edition fantasy rules from the System Reference Document 5.2.1, run by a rules engine that rolls every die in view of the table. The storyteller describes what the dice decided and never the other way round.

The look

Every scene, shot like a fantasy drama

Places, people and the things you pick up are pictured as you reach them, in a cinematic, photographic style, with music underneath. It plays like an episode your friends are starring in.

The campaign

A world that remembers

Who you met, what you promised and what you broke all stay true from one night to the next. Wander off the path and the story follows you.

Questions

What you need

  • A TV, monitor or laptop for the shared screen
  • A phone, tablet or laptop for each player
  • A modern browser and an internet connection
  • Friends. Snacks are optional.
Is an AI Dungeon Master any good?

It’s early access, so judge it on your free $10. What we can promise: a rules engine, not the storyteller, rolls the dice and tracks the rules, so the AI can’t fudge a roll or forget your hit points. The storyteller handles the voices, the descriptions and what the world does next.

Does everyone need an account?

Only the host who runs the table. Players join with the room code on the TV and pay nothing.

Do we need to know the rules?

No. Your phone offers the moves that make sense right now and shows your odds before you commit. The rules engine handles the rest, and veterans will find the fifth-edition rules they know.

How far does $10 go?

It depends on how you play: picture quality, how often scenes change and whether characters speak aloud. Your account page estimates the play time left at your settings, so you always know before you start. We will publish typical costs per session once early access gives us real numbers.

What happens when the credit runs out?

The moment in progress finishes, then the table pauses with a prompt to top up. Your campaign, your heroes and everything that happened are saved.

Can a bad roll ruin the night?

Bad rolls have real consequences, because that is what makes good rolls matter. The story always offers a way forward, and the narration can never change a result the dice decided.

What happens to what we say and type?

What your table says and types is sent to the AI services that run the story, the voices and the pictures, so they can answer you. Our privacy policy lists those services; it is a draft until launch.
