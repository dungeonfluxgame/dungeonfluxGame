Content policy

A plain-language draft of the policy we plan to publish. It will be replaced by the final text after counsel reviews it.

Draft for review by counsel. Not yet in effect.

This page explains how the policy is planned to work, in plain language. It is not a contract, it does not bind anyone, and it may change. Drafted 24 October 2026.

In short

  • DungeonFlux is an adult service for fantasy adventure play.
  • Explicit sexual content, copying a real person’s voice without permission, and protected likenesses are not offered at launch.
  • You may upload only what you have the right to use.
  • The rules come from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 and DungeonFlux is not official Dungeons & Dragons.

What this covers

The same content rules apply to text, pictures, voice and video. The launch content profile is meant to be acceptable to the payment, AI and hosting providers we depend on, and it may be narrower than what the law allows.

Not offered at launch

  • Explicit sexual content.
  • Copying a real person’s voice without authorisation.
  • Generating protected likenesses.

Uploads

Character portraits and other files you upload must be yours or be used with permission. Uploads are checked for type, size and safety before they are used, and rejected files are removed. We record the permissions you give for uploaded media.

AI and player input

What players type or say is treated as story input. It cannot unlock restricted content, authorise spending or override provider rules. A request such as “generate a thousand videos” does not bypass the table’s settings.

Reports, takedowns and appeals

The process for reporting content, suspending accounts, appeals, takedowns and law-enforcement requests is still to be defined with counsel.
