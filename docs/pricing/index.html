Pricing

You pay for the time your table is actively playing, plus the AI work that makes the voices, pictures and narration. There is one table fee, not one per player.

Proposed pilot pricing, subject to change

These prices have not been approved, you cannot buy credit yet, and nothing on this page is a contract. Rates, presets and allowances may change before launch.

The short version

Who pays?
One table fee, not one per player. Anyone at the table can chip in, shares follow what each person authorised, and unused credit goes back to the person who put it in.
What if the money runs out?
The table pauses, the campaign is kept, and nothing is deleted. Play picks up when someone adds funding.
How do I start?
New accounts will get $10 of promotional credit once their email is confirmed. Buying credit is not open yet.

$10

Promotional credit for new accounts

Every new account with a confirmed email will get $10 of promotional credit to play. It has no cash value, it cannot be turned into purchased credit, and it pays for play on DungeonFlux only. One per account.

The table fee

The platform rate is $2 per active table-hour.

  • It is shared by the whole table. Adding a player does not add a fee.
  • It is metered by exact time, with no one-hour minimum. Two hours and fifteen minutes of play is $4.50.
  • The clock runs only while the table is actively playing. The lobby, a host pause, and time lost to a blocked AI service are free.
  • Party size changes how the cost is split, not the rate. A bigger party can use more AI work, and that shows up in the AI line.

AI work and the 1.5× rate

AI work is charged at 1.5 times the published price of the vendor service behind it.

  • You see it in plain categories: spoken narration, microphone transcription, scene pictures, character portraits, music and optional cinematics.
  • The price is fixed for a session. If a vendor changes its price in the middle of your night, your session keeps the price it started with.
  • Saving, viewing characters, viewing pictures you already have and ordinary export cost no new AI charge. Replaying a finished scene is never charged again.
  • A new portrait, a new scene picture or a redo is quoted first, or covered by the automatic-picture setting you approved.
  • Premium cinematics are off by default. You see the maximum price and who pays, and nothing is made until someone approves. The button says what it does: “Generate for up to $X.”
  • We do not bill you for our own development and testing, defects, duplicate requests, or pictures made in advance and never used.

Spending ceilings and presets

A ceiling is a limit, not a target. The table sets how much can be spent in any rolling hour, how much for the whole session, how much one person can lose, and the size above which a single request needs approval. Work already in progress counts against the ceiling.

Estimated, maximum authorised and actually charged

These are three different numbers, and the product shows them separately.

Estimated
Our forecast for your settings, shown as a range from recent use and the funding available. Because a ceiling allows bursts, a balance divided by a rate is not a promise of how many hours remain.
Maximum authorised
The most you and the table have allowed: the hourly ceiling, the session ceiling, your own limit and any request you approved. Spending is held below it, and work in progress counts against it.
Actually charged
What the usage statement shows once work has settled: exact time and delivered results, never above the quote, with failures credited.

How billing works

A night, step by step
  1. 7:00 pmFriends join the lobby. Nothing is charged.
  2. 7:10 pmThe host starts the session. The table clock starts.
  3. 7:50 pmThe host pauses for pizza. The clock stops. That is 40 minutes of play so far.
  4. 8:05 pmPlay resumes. The clock starts again.
  5. 8:40 pmOne scene picture fails to generate. The request is credited, and play carries on.
  6. 9:25 pmThe host ends the session. The clock stops at 2 hours of play in total.

Each person then sees a statement. The whole night, with AI vendor prices of $2.00 for narration and $1.00 for pictures, looks like this.

Adding credit

Credit is planned as one-off top-ups. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

  • The amounts planned are $10, $20 and $50, plus a bounded custom amount.
  • You see the credit you receive, any tax, and the exact total before you pay. Card details are entered on the payment processor’s page and never reach DungeonFlux.
  • Credit is for DungeonFlux services only. It is not cash and cannot be transferred between people or withdrawn.
  • The payment processor and counsel have not yet approved this credit structure. It may change, for example to fixed-price purchases for each session, and this page would change with it.
