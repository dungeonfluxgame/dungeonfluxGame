Pricing

One price for the whole table, not one per player. You pick a pace, and you are charged only for what your table uses, never more than that pace per hour.

The short version

Who pays?
One price for the whole table, not one per player. Anyone at the table can chip in, shares follow what each person authorised, and unused credit goes back to the person who put it in.
What if the money runs out?
The table pauses, the campaign is kept, and nothing is deleted. Play picks up when someone adds funding.
How do I start?
New accounts will get $10 of promotional credit once their email is confirmed. Buying credit is not open yet.

$10

Promotional credit for new accounts

Every new account with a confirmed email will get $10 of promotional credit to play. It has no cash value, it cannot be turned into purchased credit, and it pays for play on DungeonFlux only. One per account.

What you pay

  • One price covers the whole table. Adding a player does not add a charge.
  • You are charged for what the table uses, measured by exact time, never more than your pace per hour and with no one-hour minimum.
  • You see an estimate and the maximum before you start, and the actual charge afterwards.
  • A session’s price is fixed once it starts. A price change later does not reprice a session that is already running.
  • Pauses, failures and errors are not charged. The table below shows what happens in each case.
  • Saving, viewing characters, viewing pictures you already have and ordinary export cost nothing extra. Replaying a finished scene is never charged again.
  • A new portrait, a new scene picture or a redo is quoted first, or covered by the automatic-picture setting you approved.
  • Premium cinematics are off by default. You see the maximum price and who pays, and nothing is made until someone approves. The button says what it does: “Generate for up to $X.”
  • You are not billed for our own development and testing, defects, duplicate requests, or pictures made in advance and never used.

Limits you control

Your pace is a limit, not a target. The table sets how much can be spent in any rolling hour, how much for the whole session, how much one person can lose, and the size above which a single request needs approval. Work already in progress counts against the limit.

If good play does not fit inside a pace, we will change or remove the pace rather than quietly spend more.

Estimated, maximum authorised and actually charged

These are three different numbers, and the product shows them separately.

Estimated
Our forecast for your settings, shown as a range from recent use and the funding available. Because a limit allows bursts, a balance divided by a rate is not a promise of how many hours remain.
Maximum authorised
The most you and the table have allowed: the hourly limit, the session limit, your own limit and any request you approved. Spending is held below it, and work in progress counts against it.
Actually charged
What the usage statement shows once work has settled: exact time and delivered results, never above the quote, with failures credited.

How billing works

A night, step by step
  1. LobbyFriends join. Nothing is charged.
  2. StartThe host starts the session. The table clock starts.
  3. PauseThe host pauses for pizza. The clock stops.
  4. ResumePlay resumes. The clock starts again.
  5. FailureOne scene picture fails to generate. The request is credited, and play carries on.
  6. EndThe host ends the session. The clock stops.

Each person then sees a statement for the night. Here is one, at the Immersive pace.

Unused purchased credit can be refunded to the original payment method under the proposed pilot policy. Promotional credit has no cash value. The details are in the refund policy draft and the credit terms draft.

Prices exclude applicable taxes. Where tax applies, checkout shows it before you pay, so the credit you receive and the total charged are both stated.

Adding credit

Credit is planned as one-off top-ups. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

  • The amounts planned are $10, $20 and $50, plus a bounded custom amount.
  • You see the credit you receive, any tax, and the exact total before you pay. Card details are entered on the payment processor’s page and never reach DungeonFlux.
  • Credit is for DungeonFlux services only. It is not cash and cannot be transferred between people or withdrawn.
  • The payment processor and counsel have not yet approved this credit structure. It may change, for example to fixed-price purchases for each session, and this page would change with it.
