# The landing page (Home screen): exactly three components over the hall plate, assembled one at a
# time, then changed field by field. The menu list is the navigation rail drawn as a plain list, the
# Play medallion is the one primary action, and the past campaigns are the campaign list. The brand
# mark, the title block, the top bar, the settings panel and Tonight's Adventure are not on this page
# (they stay in the code for later screens and answer to `scene home brand|title|top|configure|adventure`).
# Needs the playground host running (df-table serve) and the pictures in assets/playground/media.
playground go ui_home
scene reset
# Start from the bare hall: take away the whole page and the pills the fixture does not draw.
scene home clear
scene pill clear balance
scene pill clear rate
scene pill clear profile
wait 2500
# The Play medallion first: the big central button, with the campaign it continues as its subtitle.
scene home actions 1 label=Play subtitle="The Ashen Citadel" glyph=play variant=primary
wait 2500
# The menu list at the left, entry by entry; the number after the last adds an entry.
scene home nav 1 label=Continue glyph=play
wait 700
scene home nav 2 label="New Adventure" glyph=sparkle
wait 700
scene home nav 3 label="Join Session" glyph=players
wait 700
scene home nav 4 label=Characters glyph=characters
wait 700
scene home nav 5 label=Settings glyph=settings
wait 1800
# The past campaigns at the right: three entries, the first one selected.
scene home campaigns set title="Past Campaigns"
wait 1200
scene home campaigns card-add "The Ashen Citadel" art=campaign-ashen-citadel.webp last="Played yesterday" selected=true
wait 900
scene home campaigns card-add "Neon Hollow" art=campaign-neon-alley.webp last="Played 3 days ago"
wait 900
scene home campaigns card-add "Crown of the Last Machine" art=campaign-ringed-station.webp last="Played last week"
wait 3000
# The focus: the remote starts on Play, moves down the menu, across to the campaigns, and returns.
scene home focus nav:1
wait 1500
scene home focus nav:2
wait 1500
scene home focus nav:3
wait 1500
scene home focus campaigns:2
wait 1800
scene home focus actions:1
wait 2000
scene home focus none
wait 1500
# Change one field at a time: the Play words, an entry's name, an entry switched off.
scene home actions 1 subtitle="Neon Hollow"
wait 2200
scene home actions 1 subtitle="The Ashen Citadel"
wait 1500
scene home actions 1 label=Resume
wait 2200
scene home actions 1 label=Play
wait 1500
scene home nav 2 label="New Campaign"
wait 2200
scene home nav 2 label="New Adventure"
wait 1500
scene home actions 1 enabled=false
wait 2500
scene home actions 1 enabled=true
wait 1500
# The selection (the campaign that will continue) and the focus (where the remote is) are separate.
scene home campaigns select 3
wait 2200
scene home campaigns select 1
wait 1500
scene home campaigns card 2 last="Played today"
wait 2200
scene home campaigns card 2 last="Played 3 days ago"
wait 1200
scene home campaigns set title="Pick up where you left off"
wait 2200
scene home campaigns set title="Past Campaigns"
wait 1500
# An entry goes and comes back; a campaign goes and comes back.
scene home nav remove 5
wait 2000
scene home nav 5 label=Settings glyph=settings
wait 1500
scene home campaigns card-remove 3
wait 2000
scene home campaigns card-add "Crown of the Last Machine" art=campaign-ringed-station.webp last="Played last week"
wait 2500
