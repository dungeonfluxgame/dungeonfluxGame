# The Director's camera: directions with reasons, what holds them back, and the return.
# Needs the playground host running (df-table serve) and the avatar kind (assets/canvas/kinds).
# The Director has a clock of its own and `director advance` moves it, so a hold ends and a
# waiting direction starts without any wait here.
playground go camera_demo
scene reset
clear
spawn avatar finn x=30 y=30 name=Finn color=#d9a441 portrait=finn-alder.webp
spawn avatar sigrun x=70 y=26 name=Sigrun color=#7da35b portrait=sigrun.webp
spawn avatar lurker x=50 y=40 name=Lurker color=#555555
director view clear
director view hero finn
director view hide lurker
camera focus finn reason=speaker
camera focus sigrun reason=speaker
director advance 800
camera focus lurker reason=hit
camera focus sigrun reason=hit
director show
director end hit sigrun
director advance 1000
camera focus finn reason=reveal hold=2000
director advance 2000
director view hide sigrun
director view unhide sigrun
director advance 1000
camera focus sigrun reason=arrival
director view hide sigrun
director show
director view clear
