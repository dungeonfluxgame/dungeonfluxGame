# Security policy

## Supported versions

The supported version is `main`. There are no packaged releases yet.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues or pull requests. Use
GitHub's private vulnerability reporting through **Report a vulnerability** on the
repository's Security tab, or email [support@dungeonfluxdnd.com](mailto:support@dungeonfluxdnd.com).

Include the affected component or revision, the conditions needed to reproduce the
issue, its possible impact and any safe proof of concept. Do not include real credentials,
private player data or other people's information. If possible, use a minimal demonstration
with test data.

The maintainers intend to acknowledge reports within five business days and to share an
initial assessment within ten business days. These are response targets, not guarantees;
complex reports may take longer to assess or resolve.
