SUBJECT: Your payment is confirmed

BODY:
Hello {{display_name}},

We received your payment on {{purchase_date}}. Receipt {{receipt_id}}.

Service credit added: {{credit_amount}}.
Tax: {{tax_amount}}.
Total charged to {{payment_method_label}}: {{total_charged}}.

Your available purchased credit is now {{available_credit_after}}.

Service credit cannot be transferred to another person. Refund terms are at {{terms_url}}.

View this receipt and your usage at {{receipt_url}}.

PLACEHOLDERS:
{{display_name}}: Recipient's display name.
{{purchase_date}}: When the processor's verified webhook confirmed the payment, in the recipient's time zone.
{{receipt_id}}: Receipt number for the purchase.
{{credit_amount}}: Service credit granted by this purchase, for example $20.00.
{{tax_amount}}: Tax on the purchase, for example $0.00. The tax specialist sets the rule and the wording (plan 6.7).
{{payment_method_label}}: Card brand and last four digits only, for example "Visa ending 6411". Nothing else about the card.
{{total_charged}}: Exact amount charged, for example $21.00.
{{available_credit_after}}: Purchased service credit available after this purchase. Excludes promotional credit.
{{terms_url}}: Plain address of the terms and refund page.
{{receipt_url}}: Plain address of the receipt page.

NOTE:
Plan section: 9.8 (add-credit page; clarify nontransferability and eligible refund terms), 9.7 (receipts and purchase lines kept separate from allocations), 7.2 (a bank receipt documents a purchase), 11 (payment confirmed). Journey J01.
Send only after the verified webhook is recorded and the purchase is credited. The redirect from Checkout alone never confirms a purchase (acceptance test PAY-01).
Not a tax invoice: the tax line is supplied as the tax specialist decides (plan 6.7). Do not change the wording of this template without that review.
Never include the processor's session ID, payment intent ID, billing address or any card number. The payment method label is the card suffix only.
