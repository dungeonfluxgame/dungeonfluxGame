Send only after funds are confirmed and the allocation is committed.
Show only the recipient's own amounts. Other payers' amounts and balances are not in this message (plan 5.3 allows shared names and amounts only under an explicit group setting, which this template does not use).
Consent version and timestamp are recorded in the app, not in this email.
