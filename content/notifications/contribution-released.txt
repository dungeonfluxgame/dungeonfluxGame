PLACEHOLDERS:
{{display_name}}: Recipient's display name.
{{campaign_name}}: Campaign name.
{{session_name}}: Session name or number as members see it.
{{session_end_time}}: When the host ended the session, in the recipient's time zone.
{{released_amount}}: Unused allocation returned to the recipient's available service credit, for example $8.00.
{{unresolved_amount}}: Amount still held for work with a pending or unknown outcome, for example $0.70. Use $0.00 when nothing is held.
{{billing_url}}: Plain address of the billing page.
