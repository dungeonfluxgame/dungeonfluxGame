SUBJECT: Your data export is ready

BODY:
Hello {{display_name}},

Your export of {{export_scope}} is ready. It was prepared at {{prepared_time}}. File size: {{file_size}}.

Download it at {{download_link}}. The link stops working at {{link_expires_at}}.

Anyone with this link can download the file until then, so do not share it. Each download is recorded.

If you did not ask for this export, sign in at {{account_security_url}}, change your password, and contact {{support_address}}.

PLACEHOLDERS:
{{display_name}}: Recipient's display name.
{{export_scope}}: Either "your account" or "the campaign {{campaign_name}}", as the export requires. The caller fills in the full phrase.
{{campaign_name}}: Campaign name, used only when the export scope is a campaign. Empty for an account export.
{{prepared_time}}: When the export was built, in the recipient's time zone.
{{file_size}}: Approximate size of the file, for example 3.4 MB.
{{download_link}}: Short-lived download link. Credential-bearing (see NOTE).
{{link_expires_at}}: Expiry time of the download link, in the recipient's time zone.
{{account_security_url}}: Plain address of the account security page. No token and no query string.
{{support_address}}: Support contact address.

NOTE:
Plan section: 13.11 (short-lived private download, access recorded, archive expires; exports are audience-filtered and exclude other people's data), J14 (request, reauthenticate, short-lived download) and 11 (export ready).
Research: planning/account-service-research.md 7.5 (account export link valid for 24 hours). The expiry is set by configuration, not by this text.
Short-lived link: {{download_link}} is the only credential in this template, required by plan 13.11 and J14. Render it at send time, never log the rendered body, and keep it out of analytics (plan 11).
Plan 13.11 requires reauthentication for large or private exports before download. This email does not replace that step.
Do not put export contents in this message.
