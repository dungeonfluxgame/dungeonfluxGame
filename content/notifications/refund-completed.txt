SUBJECT: Your refund of {{refund_amount}} is complete

BODY:
Hello {{display_name}},

Your refund of {{refund_amount}} for receipt {{receipt_id}} was completed on {{completed_time}}.

It went to {{payment_method_label}}, the original payment method. How quickly the money appears depends on your bank.

Refund reference: {{refund_reference}}.

Your available purchased credit is now {{available_credit_after}}.

PLACEHOLDERS:
{{display_name}}: Recipient's display name.
{{refund_amount}}: Cash amount refunded, for example $12.40.
{{receipt_id}}: Receipt number of the refunded purchase.
{{completed_time}}: When the processor reported the refund as succeeded, in the recipient's time zone.
{{payment_method_label}}: Card brand and last four digits only, for example "Visa ending 6411".
{{refund_reference}}: Refund reference for support and status pages.
{{available_credit_after}}: Purchased service credit still available after the refund. Excludes promotional credit.

NOTE:
Plan section: 6.7 (the refund finalizes only after the definitive processor status), 9.7 (completed is shown only after that status), 11 (refund updates) and J13.
Send only on the processor's definitive success, never on request creation or on acceptance alone.
Promotional (trial) credit is never refunded (research decision 20), so it does not appear in this message.
