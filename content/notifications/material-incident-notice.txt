SUBJECT: Important: {{incident_title}}

BODY:
Hello {{display_name}},

We are writing about an incident on {{incident_date}} that affected {{affected_scope}}.

What happened: {{incident_summary}}

Information involved: {{data_categories}}.

What we have done: {{actions_taken}}.

What you should do: {{customer_actions}}.

{{financial_impact}}

We will write again by {{next_update_time}}. Questions: {{support_address}}.

We will never ask for your password or card details by email.

PLACEHOLDERS:
{{display_name}}: Recipient's display name.
{{incident_title}}: Short plain title, for example "sign-in problem on 24 Oct 2026".
{{incident_date}}: Date or date range of the incident.
{{affected_scope}}: Plain description of who or what was affected, for example "accounts that uploaded portraits".
{{incident_summary}}: Two or three plain sentences. Counsel reviews the wording. No internal system names and no exploit detail.
{{data_categories}}: Categories of personal or game data involved, in plain words. Write "none" if none.
{{actions_taken}}: Plain description of the response.
{{customer_actions}}: Specific steps for the recipient, or "none required".
{{financial_impact}}: One sentence on any credit, refund or hold affected, or "Your balance and purchases are not affected."
{{next_update_time}}: When the next update will be sent, in the recipient's time zone.
{{support_address}}: Support contact address.

NOTE:
Plan section: 11 (a material incident notice is a transactional notification) and 16 (secret compromise and storage incidents: assess notification obligations; deny missing output and provide customer credit when purchased output cannot be delivered).
This template carries no link placeholder. If a link is needed, use a plain page address and record the reason in the incident review.
The rendered notice must never contain secrets, tokens, provider credentials, signed URLs or payment instruments (plan 11).
Counsel and the incident owner approve each incident's wording before it goes out. This file is a skeleton, not approved text (legal review is a paid-launch gate).
Recipients: affected accounts only, as chosen by the incident owner.
